You can now customize Siri’s pace and expressivity in the latest iOS 27 beta

Apple released the second beta of iOS 27 for developers this week. Among the changes is a new option to adjust how Siri speaks. Users can now control the speed and expressiveness of the assistant’s voice directly from Settings.

What You Need to Know

The setting appears under Settings > Accessibility > Siri > Speech Rate and Expressivity. Two sliders let you change the speaking pace from 0.5x to 2.0x the default speed and adjust the expressivity level from flat to highly animated.

Moving the sliders modifies pitch, tempo, and the emphasis placed on certain words. The preview button plays a short sample sentence so you can hear the effect before applying it system‑wide.

Any adjustment you make affects all Siri responses, including those triggered by Hey Siri, the Siri interface, and third‑party apps that use Apple’s speech synthesis API. The feature is only present in the current developer beta and will be included in a future public release.

Why It Matters

Giving users control over how fast and how expressively Siri talks improves accessibility for people who need slower speech to follow along or who prefer a more lively tone for clarity. It also lets anyone tailor the assistant to match their personal listening preferences.

Apple says the change is part of a broader effort to make Siri feel more natural and personal as the assistant is rebuilt around generative AI models. By exposing low‑level speech controls, the company aims to reduce the robotic feel that some users have reported.

Key Details

  • Located in Settings → Accessibility → Siri → Speech Rate and Expressivity
  • Speech rate slider range: 0.5x (half speed) to 2.0x (double speed)
  • Expressivity slider range: flat (monotone) to highly animated (varied pitch and emphasis)
  • Preview button plays a sample sentence using the current slider values
  • Changes apply to all system Siri responses and any app using Apple’s speech synthesis API
  • Only available in the iOS 27 developer beta; public rollout expected after testing

What's Next

Apple will likely gather feedback from beta testers before refining the sliders for the final iOS 27 release. Future updates may add more granular controls, such as independent pitch adjustment or voice‑style presets, further integrating the generative AI improvements that underlie the new Siri experience.
