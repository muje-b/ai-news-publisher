        The paper introduces MR‑Traj, a multi‑resolution diffusion model that builds trajectories layer by layer. At the coarsest level, the model samples a low‑resolution sketch of a path, capturing the general origin‑destination direction and major waypoints. Subsequent diffusion stages refine this sketch, adding intermediate points at progressively higher spatial resolution while respecting temporal ordering.

        Paragraph 2:

        Training uses a hierarchical noise schedule: each resolution receives its own diffusion process, allowing the network to learn both long‑range movement trends and short‑range maneuvers such as turns or stops. The generated trajectories are evaluated against real‑world mobility benchmarks, showing improved preservation of route similarity, speed distributions, and stop‑frequency patterns compared with single‑scale baselines.
