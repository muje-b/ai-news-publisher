      Why It Matters

      Smart speakers have struggled to move beyond basic utilities like timers, music playback, and light switches. Consumers keep them on counters mainly for convenience, not for sophisticated interaction. If the voice assistant can truly understand context and handle complex requests, the device could become a hub for information, coaching, and home automation rather than just a speaker.

      Google’s hardware is strong enough to support that shift, but the software gap means the promise remains unfulfilled. Until Gemini matures, buyers may choose to stick with existing speakers or look at competitors whose assistants are more reliable, even if their hardware is less impressive.
